About

I am an Assistant Professor of Marketing at the Jaipuria Institute of Management, Lucknow (since January 2023). I hold a Ph.D. in Marketing from Banaras Hindu University and an MBA in International Business from Devi Ahilya Vishwavidyalaya. I am a proud alumnus of the 46th Faculty Development Programme at IIM Ahmedabad (April–May 2026).

My research centres on consumer behaviour, healthcare marketing, and management education, employing structural equation modelling, CFA, PLS-SEM, and qualitative methods. My work appears in Journal of Health Organization and Management, International Journal of Management Education, International Journal of Quality and Service Sciences, and other Scopus Q1–Q2 journals.

🏛 IIMA Alumni — 46th FDP in Pedagogy & Research Methods Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad  ·  April 7 – May 21, 2026

I serve as a member of the AIDEDU ERASMUS+ EU Project (2023–ongoing), an international initiative on inclusive digitized education, with visits to institutions in Nepal and Spain. I also conduct Management Development Programmes for organisations including BPCL, IOCL, and Annpurna Finance.
